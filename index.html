Sri Lanka is a beautiful island nation in the Indian Ocean, famous for its natural beauty, rich culture, and warm hospitality. Every year, thousands of tourists visit the country to enjoy its golden beaches, green mountains, ancient cities, and wildlife. Popular tourist destinations include Sigiriya Rock Fortress, the Temple of the Tooth in Kandy, Ella, Nuwara Eliya, Galle Fort, and Yala National Park. Visitors can also enjoy exciting activities such as hiking, surfing, whale watching, and safari tours. Sri Lanka is well known for its delicious traditional food, colourful festivals, and world-famous Ceylon tea. The country's diverse climate allows tourists to experience both sunny beaches and cool hill country in a single trip. Tourism plays an important role in Sri Lanka's economy by creating jobs and supporting local businesses such as hotels, restaurants, transport services, and handicraft shops.
